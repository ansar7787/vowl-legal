REFUND POLICY

Last Updated: September 2026

1. REFUND ELIGIBILITY

We want you to be completely satisfied with your Vowl Premium experience. If you are not satisfied, you may request a refund within 7 days of your initial purchase, provided that you have not significantly consumed the premium features.

2. NON-REFUNDABLE ITEMS

After the 7-day period has passed, or if you have fully utilized purchased Coin Packs, the purchase is considered final and non-refundable. Auto-renewing subscriptions must be canceled before the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next cycle.

3. PROCESSING TIME

If your purchase was made via Razorpay, approved refunds will be processed immediately and will automatically reflect in your original payment method (Bank Account, UPI, or Credit Card) within 5 to 7 business days depending on your bank.

4. AUTOMATIC REFUNDS (FAILED TRANSACTIONS)

If your payment is successfully deducted from your bank but fails to activate your Premium account due to a network error, Razorpay will automatically detect this dropped transaction and issue a refund to your original payment method within 5 days.

5. HOW TO REQUEST A REFUND

To request a manual refund, please email our support team with your account email and your Razorpay Transaction ID (which is sent to your email after purchase).
